How to cite

The OmniMouse dataset builds on experimental data from multiple projects. When using it for scientific purposes, please cite:

  1. the original data source(s) your results depend on and the corresponding paper,
  2. and, if possible, the OmniMouse dataset repository/homepage.

The corresponding paper for each individual experiment is linked from the Dataset Details page. Click any button below to open the BibTeX citation.
