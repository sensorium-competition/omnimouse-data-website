OmniMouse Dataset

One of the largest single-neuron datasets to date. Visual stimuli, neural responses, and aligned behavior from awake, head-fixed mice, combining newly recorded data with recordings from prior projects.

This dataset was collected in the laboratory of Prof. Andreas Tolias.

Dataset DOI: 10.57967/hf/8529 · See the How to cite page for the BibTeX entry.
